OpenAI realtime firmware for the Voice PE

Firmware for the Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition that talks to the OpenAI Realtime Voice Agent add-on in place of Assist's own voice pipeline.

Installing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer: this browser can't reach USB devices. Installing needs a secure (https) page.

Connect the Voice PE to this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data, then press Install.

After installing

  1. Wi-Fi. The installer offers to connect the Voice PE to your Wi-Fi when it's done. You can also do it later over Bluetooth: Home Assistant finds the Voice PE nearby, and you press its centre button when asked.
  2. Home Assistant. Add the Voice PE when Home Assistant discovers it (Settings → Devices & services).
  3. The add-on. With the add-on running and its integration installed, Home Assistant also discovers the integration, as OpenAI Realtime Voice Agent. Add it, and pick the Voice PE when it asks: the integration links the Voice PE to the add-on. The first time, Home Assistant asks you to press the Voice PE's centre button to accept the link: its lights twinkle until you do. Press it only when Home Assistant asks, not because the lights twinkle.

Updates come to Home Assistant as a firmware update for the Voice PE, from this page's releases. The full instructions are in INSTALL.md.
